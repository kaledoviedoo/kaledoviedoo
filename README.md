## About

I design and build full-stack systems end to end — backend services, data pipelines, and the interfaces on top. Most of my work centers on local inference and self-hosted architectures: FastAPI services orchestrating local LLMs, structured validation layers for unreliable inputs (speech, unstructured documents), and data models built to hold up under real use, not just a demo.

- Studying Systems Engineering at Escuela Colombiana de Ingeniería Julio Garavito
- Designing local-first AI systems (voice, speech analytics, financial signal extraction) with proper service boundaries instead of a single script calling an API
- Interested in data structures, automation, and applied finance
- Also tinker with hardware: Raspberry Pi, 3D printing, and robotics on the side

## What I work on

Most of my projects sit at the intersection of local-first AI and practical automation. A few threads that keep showing up:

- **Voice and language systems** — local voice assistants and speech/linguistic analysis pipelines: audio ingestion, transcription, LLM-assisted classification, and structured validation on top of local inference (Ollama) instead of a hosted API.
- **Applied data and finance** — pipelines that parse unstructured public documents (central bank releases, government reports), extract structured signals, and map them against financial assets to produce something usable downstream.
- **Industry-specific SaaS** — full platforms like a 3D digital twin for agro-industrial management, where the hard part is the data model and predictive layer, not just the UI on top.

I gravitate toward projects where I own the whole pipeline — ingestion, processing, storage, and the interface — and I care more about getting the architecture right than shipping something that only works in the happy path.

Check out my [pinned repositories](https://github.com/kaledoviedoo?tab=repositories) for the current projects.
